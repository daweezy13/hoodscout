Method & caveats

TVL, stablecoins and app fees from DefiLlama; active users and gas from Blockscout; memecoins from DexScreener cross-checked against GeckoTerminal; NFT volume by decoding Seaport OrderFulfilled logs off the chain RPC; holder payouts from DividendsDistributed events and booster transfers, both read on-chain.

Gas fees and app fees are separate numbers — paid to the chain versus earned by protocols on it, ~30x apart, never summed. The stablecoin split is an approximation: DefiLlama publishes per-chain history only in aggregate, so the USDG/USDe division is held constant backwards. Daily is the finest resolution available. A matching contract name is not proof of authenticity — it is exactly what a copycat is built to have.

Independently cross-checked against Dune: 13 of 13 checks agree, worst divergence 1.7%. Active users is count(distinct sender) per UTC day and matches Blockscout exactly, which pins down that provider's otherwise undocumented definition. Checked 2026-08-12.
